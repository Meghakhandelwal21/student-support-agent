# Fictional Student Handbook — Demo Policy

## Fee payments and failed payment portal
Students should check their fee status in the student portal. If the payment portal fails, save the error message or screenshot and contact the Student Accounts Office. Whether a payment can be made on the next working day depends on the institution's official notice and the student's deadline. This demo handbook does not authorize late payment or waive a penalty.

## Scholarships
Scholarship status is visible in the student support system. "Under review" means the application is still being checked; it does not mean approval. Students should contact the Scholarship Desk if the status remains unchanged beyond the published review timeline. This fictional handbook does not define a specific number of days.

## Course and programme changes
Course or programme changes require a formal request and review by the academic administration team. A request is not a completed change. Sensitive changes must not be executed automatically by the support agent and require an authorized human reviewer.

## Student privacy and account security
Students may access their own private records only. A student must not access another student's fees, scholarship details, or other private records. Passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs), API keys, and access tokens must not be shared in chat or saved in persistent memory.

## Support requests
Students may create a support request describing their issue. The system should return a ticket reference and initial status. Do not include passwords, OTPs, or payment-card details in a ticket.
